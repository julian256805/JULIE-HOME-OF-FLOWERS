HOW IT STARTED

For the love of little things.

Petal & Tide began with a bucket of garden clippings, a borrowed market table, and a hunch that flowers could make an ordinary Tuesday feel a little more like itself.

These days, we're still small by choice. We work with nearby growers whenever the season allows, keep our designs loose and natural, and make each arrangement one at a time in our Brooklyn studio.
