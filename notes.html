A NOTE TO KEEP

Make your flowers feel at home.

  1. 01

    Give your vase a good wash before filling it with cool, fresh water.

  2. 02

    Trim each stem at an angle, then remove any leaves that sit below the waterline.

  3. 03

    Refresh the water every couple of days and keep the arrangement out of direct sun.
